End-to-end legal support for M&A deals, including legal due diligence and transaction structuring.

CY Botax & Partners provides comprehensive legal support for Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A) across a wide range of industries. Our team of experienced attorneys advises clients throughout the entire transaction lifecycle, from initial deal structuring and legal due diligence to negotiation and post-closing integration.

We work closely with buyers, sellers, and strategic partners to maximize deal value while managing complex legal and regulatory risks in a dynamic business environment.

⬡

Post-merger integration (PMI) legal support and corporate restructuring
